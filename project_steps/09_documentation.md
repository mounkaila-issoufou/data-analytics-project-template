# Step 09 — Documentation, Delivery & Communication

## Purpose of this step
This step ensures that the project is:
- Understandable
- Reusable
- Maintainable
- Presentable to stakeholders or recruiters

The objective is to formalize knowledge and deliver results professionally.

---

## Why this step matters
A project without documentation:
- Cannot be reused
- Cannot be trusted
- Cannot be scaled
- Is hard to evaluate

Documentation is part of the deliverable, not an afterthought.

---

## Documentation scope

The following elements must be documented:

### 1. Project overview
- Business context
- Objectives
- Key questions addressed

---

### 2. Data sources
- Origin of data
- Update frequency
- Known limitations

---

### 3. Data model
- Fact and dimension tables
- Grain definition
- Key relationships

---

### 4. KPI definitions
- Explicit formulas
- Filters and assumptions
- Business meaning

---

### 5. Key insights
- Summary of findings
- Business implications
- Recommendations

---

### 6. Technical choices
- Architecture decisions
- Tools used
- Trade-offs made

---

## Directory structure

Documentation should live in:

```text
docs/
├── data_dictionary.md
├── data_model.md
├── kpi_definitions.md
├── assumptions_and_limits.md
└── architecture_overview.md
```


---

## Delivery standards

A professional delivery includes:
- Clean repository structure
- Clear README
- Reproducible steps
- Versioned code and SQL

The project should be runnable by a third party.

---

## Communication
Results should be communicated:
- Clearly
- Concisely
- With the right level of detail

Adapt communication style to:
- Technical audiences
- Business stakeholders
- Recruiters

---

## Portfolio positioning

For a portfolio:
- Highlight the problem-solving approach
- Emphasize business impact
- Explain your role and decisions
- Avoid unnecessary technical depth

The goal is to show **how you think**, not just what you built.

---

## Maintenance and evolution
Document:
- Known issues
- Possible improvements
- Future extensions

This shows long-term thinking.

---

## Output of this step
At the end of this step:
- The project is fully documented
- Results are easy to understand
- The repository is portfolio-ready
- The work reflects professional standards

This step closes the project lifecycle.
